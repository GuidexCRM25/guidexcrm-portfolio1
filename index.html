About these samples — the honest bit

These are portfolio pieces, built to show what properly done CRM work looks like when it's taken seriously end to end: data you can trust, a system shaped to the business, and a client who always knows what's happening. They're not slideshows or mockups — they're real, dependency-free web apps that run entirely in your browser.

Every person, company, message and number is fictional. Each sample keeps its data in a small, readable script and calculates every screen from it on the spot, which is why the dashboards, records and reports always agree with each other. The best way to judge the work is to click into any of the three and try to break it.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by HubSpot or any CRM vendor. The samples follow interface conventions people already know. Built as an independent demonstration of migration, CRM build, and client onboarding work.
